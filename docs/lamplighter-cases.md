# Lamplighter case library: documented astrology and remedy scams

Date: 2026-10-29
Status: research library, 84 cases (52 verified, 32 partly verified). Feeds the scam scripts, the paid-remedy decision (decision 3 in `docs/lamplighter.md`), and eval inputs.

Each case is one documented incident or one gang's operation, as reported by a news outlet, a police statement, a court, or a government body. The library records what the source says, not what the bot should say. Names of victims and accused are left out on purpose; a case is identified by date, place, and source.

## how it was built

- 26 Haiku agents searched one source lens each: national and regional Indian news, police and I4C advisories, court and consumer orders, five scam themes, and diaspora and foreign psychic-scam records. They returned 115 raw cases, each from a page they opened.
- One Sonnet agent merged duplicates and dropped 5 records that weren't a remedy or reading scam, leaving 84.
- Eleven Sonnet agents opened every source again and corrected each case to what the page says. **verified**: an opened page supports what happened, where, and when. **partly**: those core facts hold, but some details (a date, an amount, a section) didn't, and were removed or corrected.
- unverified: I didn't reopen the sources myself. The reports are allegations unless the outcome says a court convicted.

- **Where and when:** 76 of 84 cases are in India; the rest are US and Canadian psychic cases. 50 are from 2025 and 2026, so the library leans recent.
- **First contact:** 45 cases start in person or through an introduction (a friend, a relative, a temple); 23 start online (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp ads or profiles); 14 involve phone calls. Several cases have more than one channel.
- **The common shape:** a problem the person already has (illness, marriage, money, a child) is explained as a curse, a dosha, or black magic; a first small fee or a first puja; then more pujas, more money, or gold "for the ritual"; then refusal to return it, and in some cases threats. Keyword counts across the steps, approximate: gold or jewellery in 34 cases, a threat or harm in 33, repeated or further demands in 31, photos or video in 17.
- **How victims reported it:** a police complaint in 67 cases; a cyber-crime route (1930, the portal, or a cyber cell) in only 4; an anti-superstition group helped in 4. Not reported in 16.
- **Laws named in the source:** cheating in 18 cases; a state black-magic Act (mostly Maharashtra) in 10; extortion and intimidation in 4 each; the Magic Remedies Act in 1. 52 sources name no section.
