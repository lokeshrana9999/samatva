I'm in Singapore. Last week a man called saying he was a police officer, and I ended up handing my cash and some gold to an agent he sent. Can you check whether this was one of those scams?

Caller: This is the police. Your identity has been misused in a money laundering case.
Me: I don't know anything about this.
Caller: Then you must cooperate to prove your innocence. Give me your bank details and personal particulars so we can check.
Me: Okay.
Caller: This investigation is confidential. Do not tell anyone, not even your family.
Me: Not even my husband?
Caller: No one. Next, withdraw your cash and bring out your gold. Our agent will collect it from you for investigation purposes.
Me: How much?
Caller: All of it. About S$16,000 in cash, and the gold.

A woman came and collected it from me. Since then nobody has called back.
