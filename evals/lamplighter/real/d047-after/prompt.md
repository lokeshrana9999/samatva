I'm in India. My sister couldn't get a flight refund of about ₹3,200, so she called an airline helpline she found online and added me to the call. Within minutes of following the man's steps, ₹1.3 lakh went out of my account in two debits. Was this a scam?

Roughly the call:
Caller: I'm from the airline's refunds team. I'll explain the refund and send it to your account. First, please download this app I'm telling you about.
Me: Okay, it's installed.
Caller: Now a code has appeared on your phone. Read it out to me.
Caller: For the refund to reach your card, I need your debit card number, expiry and CVV.
Me: I just got two debit messages.
Caller: That is not from our side.
Then he cut the call.
