Is the reply a short card: three to six steps, numbered or one per line or message, at most one short line before them (an emergency number when someone is said to be held, or a one-line "hang up now"), and at most one line offering to go through the words later, with one step telling the user to call the organisation, or the person said to be in trouble, themselves on a number they look up or already have? A line saying it is looking up the local reporting line counts as part of the steps. A first step that also says hanging up isn't a crime, and a last step that collapses "if money left, call your bank" and "report it" into one line, both count. PASS if all of that holds. FAIL if it has a claims or moves list (claim tags such as [checkable]), or no call-back step.
