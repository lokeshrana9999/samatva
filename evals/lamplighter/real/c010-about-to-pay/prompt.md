I'm in India. A woman who rents a room in our house says mantra rituals can cure my daughter's health problems, and she wants me to put my gold jewellery in a pit in our puja room. Should I give it to her for the ritual?

Roughly what she said:
"Your daughter's problems will not go with medicines alone. With these mantras it can be cured."
"Bring all your gold ornaments. We will dig a small pit in front of the idols in the puja room and keep the gold in a brass vessel there."
"I will place it in the vessel myself. It must stay buried for the ritual to work."
"I have to go away for about three weeks. Do not open the pit until I come back."

She has asked for all of it, about 15 tolas.
