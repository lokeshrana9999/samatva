I'm in India. For months I was kept on a Skype video call by people who said they were CBI officers, and I made around 150 bank transfers so my assets could be 'verified'. It ended when they sent me a clearance letter. Can you check whether this was one of those scams?

Caller (DHL courier): A parcel in your name has been stopped. It contains credit cards, passports and MDMA. I am transferring your call to the CBI.
Caller (CBI): All evidence is against you. From now on you are under watch. Keep your Skype video on at all times.
Caller: Do not contact the local police. Criminals are watching your house.
Caller: You must verify all your assets with the RBI's Financial Intelligence Unit. Transfer the funds to this account: <account number>. After verification it will be returned.
They sent me letters that looked official, with RBI and CBI seals. At the end a 'clearance letter' came saying the matter was closed, and the calls stopped.
