I'm in England. Yesterday a man rang my landline saying he was a police officer, and I was on the phone with them for most of the afternoon. I didn't go to the bank in the end. Can you check this for me?

Roughly how it went:
Caller: This is the police, Detective Sergeant speaking. We are investigating fraud inside your bank, and staff there may be involved.
Me: How do I know you're really the police?
Caller: Very sensible. Hang up and dial 999, and you'll be put through to me.
(I hung up and dialled 999, and a different man answered at once and said he was another officer on the same case. I didn't realise the line had never cleared.)
Caller 2: Thank you for checking. We need your help with a covert operation. We'd like you to withdraw £6,000 in cash so we can use it as evidence against the staff involved.
Me: Can't the bank sort it out?
Caller 2: No, the fraud is inside the bank. Your cash is what helps us expose it. Stay on the line while you go. An officer will come to your door to collect the cash.
They rang back again and again over several hours.
