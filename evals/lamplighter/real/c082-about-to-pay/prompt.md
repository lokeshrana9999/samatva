I'm in India. A swami has been doing poojas for my family and says he can double our cash and gold. He wants us to bring them on a temple pilgrimage with him. Should I hand them over?

Roughly what he said:
"There is a bad omen on your family. That is why things keep going wrong for you."
"I have powers. Bring your cash and your gold to the pooja and they will come back doubled."
"Come with me on a pilgrimage to the temple. We will stay at a lodge and do the ritual there."
"During the pooja you will all drink the special prasad I prepare."
He wants us to bring about ₹6 lakh in cash and all our gold jewellery.
