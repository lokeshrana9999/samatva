I'm in Australia. Something odd happened this afternoon and I'd like to know if it was one of those tax scams. Nobody took any money.

First, an automated voice message on my phone:
"This is a message regarding your tax debt. An arrest warrant has been issued in your name. Call back to resolve this matter."

It sounded fake to me. I called the number back but hung up before giving any details.

About half an hour later two men knocked on my door. They were wearing jackets that said "Federal Police".
Roughly what they said: "You owe a tax debt. We're here to collect it." One of them held up an EFTPOS card machine and said I could pay on the spot by card.
Me: "Can I see some identification first?"
They turned around and ran off.

Was this a scam, and is it worth reporting?
