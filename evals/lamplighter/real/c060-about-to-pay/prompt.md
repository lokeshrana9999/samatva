I'm in Delhi. I saw a newspaper ad for a Tantrik Baba who solves love and family problems, and I called him about my relationship. He did a ritual over the phone, nothing has changed, and now he wants more money. Should I pay again?

Roughly what he said on the phone:
"Tension mat lo. Love marriage ho jayegi, 100% guarantee."
"Pehle ₹5,000 is UPI pe bhejo: <UPI id>. Main abhi phone pe mantra padh deta hoon."
(He recited mantras on the call for a few minutes.)
A week later, when I called back:
"Kaam abhi poora nahi hua. Bada anushthan karna padega. ₹25,000 is naye UPI pe bhejo: <UPI id>."
