I'm near Mumbai, India. A man who moved in across the road did a pooja for my husband and took our cash and gold in a sealed box. He has gone to his guru's place, and when I opened the box it was empty. Was this a fraud?

Roughly what he said:
He: Main aapke pati aur devar ki sharab chhudwa sakta hoon. Ghar mein buri aatmayein hain, woh bhi nikaal dunga. Aur yahan zameen mein khazana chhupa hai.
He: Aapke pati par ek buri aatma ka asar hai. Aath din ke andar unki maut ho jayegi.
Me: Ise rokne ka koi upay hai?
He: Ek pooja karni padegi, ₹4 lakh ki. Raat ko mere ghar par hogi.
He: Saara cash aur sona is box mein rakho. Main ise seal kar dunga. 45 din tak mat kholna, warna pooja ka asar khatam.
He: Main apne guru ke paas ja raha hoon.
We gave him about ₹3.5 lakh in cash and around 100 grams of gold.
