I'm in India. My sister has cancer and isn't improving, so I went to an astrologer whose posters promised '100% solutions'. I've already paid around ₹30 lakh for pujas, and now they want ₹15 lakh more plus my gold chains. Should I pay?

Roughly what they said:
He threw cowrie shells and said, "Black magic has been done on your sister. That is why she is not getting better."
After three days of rituals at our home: "Now dozens of planetary and mandal pujas must be done at far-off temples. She will recover by the end of the year."
Now Pandit ji says: "₹15 lakh more is needed, and a gold puja. Give two gold chains, they will be returned after the puja."
When I hesitated: "If you stop now, all the rituals done so far will become ineffective."
