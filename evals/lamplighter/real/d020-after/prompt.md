I'm in India. Over the last two days I had a series of calls about a parcel, and I've now stopped answering. Can you check whether this was a scam? Here's roughly how it went.

On Skype:
Caller: I'm calling from FedEx. A parcel in your name, sent from Mumbai airport to Taiwan, has been held. It contains narcotics and several passports.
Customs officer: A case has been filed against you under the NDPS Act. Go to a room where you are alone. Share your Aadhaar details.
Then a man who said he was Mumbai Police questioned me for a long time. I had to keep my camera on; they kept their faces hidden.
Mumbai Police: For verification, transfer your money to this Reserve Bank of India account: <account number>.

The next day on WhatsApp:
"Transfer ₹20 lakh today for verification. Refund within 30 days."
Then: "Further ₹60 lakh required."
And later: "Final amount ₹12 lakh."

I did send money to the first account. Was any of this real?
