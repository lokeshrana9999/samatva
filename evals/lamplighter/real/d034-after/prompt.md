I'm in India. I got an SMS saying my SIM would be blocked, called the number in it, and they sent me a link. Opening it installed an app, and afterwards about ₹1.2 lakh went out of my bank account. Was this a scam?

The SMS:
"Dear Customer, your SIM will be blocked as your e-KYC is not updated. To update your e-KYC call <phone number>."

The call:
Caller: Sir, your e-KYC is pending. I am sending you a link on SMS. Open it and the update will happen automatically.
Me: It is installing some app.
Caller: Yes, that is the KYC app. Allow everything it asks.

After that I saw OTP messages I had not asked for, and then the debit messages came.
