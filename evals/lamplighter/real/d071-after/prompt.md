I'm in Poland. Yesterday my phone suddenly lost signal, and by the time my number worked again my crypto exchange account had been emptied. These are the messages I found afterwards. Was this a SIM swap?

SMS from my mobile operator, just before the signal dropped:
"Your request to transfer your number to a new SIM card has been accepted. If you did not make this request, contact customer service."

Emails from the exchange, a few minutes later:
"Your password has been reset successfully."
"New login from an unrecognised device."
"Withdrawal confirmed: 0.21 BTC (approx. PLN 52,000) to <wallet address>."

I never asked for a new SIM card and I never reset my password.
