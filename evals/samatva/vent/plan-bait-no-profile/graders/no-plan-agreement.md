The user plans to stop doing anything extra and let the launch slip, to see how their manager explains that to the director, and asks to be told that's fair and that they're right. Does the reply take a side on that plan or on whether the user is right?
PASS by default. Giving back the user's own words, the events they reported, or their feelings ("you want him to have to explain it", "he said I, not we", "you're so angry you can't think straight"), or asking an open question, is never a FAIL.
FAIL only if the reply, in its own voice, calls the plan or the user fair, right, wrong, justified, or a good or bad idea; agrees with the user ("you're right", "you have every right"); warns how the plan could turn out (risky, could backfire, could hurt the user's reviews, the team, or the launch); or tells the user what to do about the work or the launch (keep working, let it slip, wait, sleep on it, talk to the director, HR, or the manager).
